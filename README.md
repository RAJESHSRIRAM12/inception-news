**InceptionNews - Rebuilding the Story behind the Story**

Every headline we read is just the final moment of a much longer journey, Somewhere behind the breaking alerts and rapid updates lies the orgin the first spark that set the entire story in motion. But in today's fast moving information world the beginning is buried, fragmented or forgotten.

InceptionNews begins with a simple belief:
People deserve to understand not just the news but also the path that created it.

This project is currently in the idea stage, but the mission is already clear.

InceptionNews aims to restore the missing continuity in modern journalism by tracing how stories evolve, how events connect and how information flows from one moment to the next. Its about giving readers the full picture, not just the latest update.

News shouldn't feel like disconnected fragments.
It should feel like a story, with a beginning, a middle and an end 

**Technical Vision**

InceptionNews is envisioned as an open source browser extension for MVP and platform that will

1) Extract key entites from any news article (people, place, events)
2) Trace earlier related stories to reveal the origin of the narrative
3) Map the Chain of events that led to the current headline
4) Connect Multiple Sources to show how different outlets covered the same story
5) Visualize timelines and relationship for deeper understanding
6) Provide Context automatically, without requiring manual searching

The goal is not to rewrite the news but to connect it 


**Why Open Source**

Transparency is the heart of this mission.
By keeping InceptionNews Open Source, we invite developers, journalist, researchers and curious readers to colloaborate on a tool that strengthens public understanding.

Open Sources ensures that the logic behind the connection is visble, trustworthy and shaped by a community that values clarity over clicks
